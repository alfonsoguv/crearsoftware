Hay poco escrito sobre el diseño y la experiencia de usuario en el mundo del software. Aquí os dejo una recopilación de lo mejor de esta temática.

## Diseño de interacción y usabilidad

- ["About Face 3: The essentials of interaction design"](http://www.cooper.com/journal/2007/05/about%5Fface%5F3%5Fforeword.html) by Cooper, Reimann & Cronin
- ["Don't make me think"](http://www.sensible.com/chapter.html) by Steve Krug
- ["GUI Bloopers 2.0"](http://www.gui-bloopers.com/) by Jeff Johnson
- ["The Design of Everyday Things"](http://rads.stackoverflow.com/amzn/click/0385267746) by Donald A. Norman
- ["The Humane Interface"](http://books.google.com/books?id=D39vjmLfO3kC&pg=PP1&ots=COozb41WT8&sig=gCkOwFJmwaKismBwVI-vHmLSBJY&hl=en&sa=X&oi=book%5Fresult&resnum=1&ct=result#PPP1,M1) by Jef Raskin
- ["Designing Web Usability"](http://www.useit.com/jakob/webusability/) by Jakob Nielsen
- ["Designing the Obvious"](http://rads.stackoverflow.com/amzn/click/032145345X) by Robert Hoekman Jr

## Interfaces, formularios y visualización de datos

- [Information Dashboard Design: The Effective Visual Communication of Data](http://rads.stackoverflow.com/amzn/click/0596100167) by Stephen Few
- [Web Form Design](http://www.lukew.com/resources/web%5Fform%5Fdesign.asp) by Luke Wroblewski
- [Interaction Design](http://rads.stackoverflow.com/amzn/click/0470018666) by Rogers, Preece et al.
- [Designing Interfaces](http://designinginterfaces.com/) by Jenifer Tidwell
- [The Visual Display of Quantitative Data](http://rads.stackoverflow.com/amzn/click/1893115941) by Edward Tufte's
- [User Interface Design](http://rads.stackoverflow.com/amzn/click/1893115941) for Programmers by Joel Spolsky
